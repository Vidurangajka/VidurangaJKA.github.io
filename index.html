About

I like the problems that only show up on the bench.

Most of what I work on sits where software meets physics: a control loop that has to settle, a trace that has to hold signal integrity at DDR3 speeds, a converter that has to hit its noise target in silicon.

I started with microcontrollers and PCB layout, moved into FPGAs and processor design, and I'm now working on analog and mixed-signal IC design — currently a noise-shaping SAR ADC heading for tapeout on two open-source shuttles. Along the way I've done research internships at the National University of Singapore and IIT Kanpur, and co-authored a PLL design paper accepted at IEEE SMACD 2026 in Dresden.

I also take freelance PCB work, teach Advanced Level mathematics as a volunteer, and lead R&D at OREPA. If something involves a soldering iron, an oscilloscope or a synthesis report, I'm interested.

Research Intern — Magnetic levitation & control

National University of Singapore · Division of Industrial Design, College of Design and Engineering · with Asst. Prof. Justin Moon

Publication

SMACD 2026 · Dresden, Germany · © 2026 IEEE

A 2.4 GHz LC-VCO Fractional-N Phase Locked Loop Open-Source Design in 130-nm BiCMOS

M. Thiriloganathan, S. Ranasinghe, A. Herath, R. Rameshkumar, H. Marasinghe, A. Viduranga, G. Leelarathne, K. Wickremasinghe

A 2.4 GHz type-II fractional-N PLL implemented in the IHP SG13G2 130 nm open-source BiCMOS technology, built around a cross-coupled differential LC-VCO with a custom spiral inductor characterised through an open-source electromagnetic modelling workflow.
